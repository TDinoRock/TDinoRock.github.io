2D Platformer

This 2D platformer places the player in control of an astronaut as they navigate enemy fire and collect coins to reach a target score of 100. It was my first Unity project, and it provided a strong foundation in the Unity engine, C# scripting, and core gameplay systems. The project gave me hands-on experience with design iteration, prototyping, and playtesting, and it served as an effective introduction to game development fundamentals.


Made in Unity in C# language
