Marine Training Simulator

Marine Training Simulator is a two-player local first-person shooter focused on core combat systems and player feedback. The project included the implementation of ricochet laser mechanics, shield interactions, and player-specific UI indicators for dash and firing availability. It also deepened my understanding of raycasting and iterative debugging in a fast-paced development cycle. The experience reinforced the importance of systems design, usability, and rapid iteration in creating responsive and engaging gameplay.


Made in Unity in C# language
