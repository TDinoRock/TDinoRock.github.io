My name is Trennon Talbot, and I am a senior at Boise State University.

I am a game designer and creative technologist with a passion for building immersive interactive experiences, designing engaging systems, and bringing ideas to life through both gameplay and visual storytelling.

This portfolio highlights my work across game design, 3D art, animation, and interactive media, with a focus on creating meaningful player experiences that balance fun, challenge, and polish.

My recent work includes developing Accurate Weaponry, a VR combat experience designed around realistic movement, expressive combat mechanics, and player-driven interaction.

Recent Projects

Accurate Weaponry

Accurate Weaponry is a physics-driven, open-world VR combat simulator focused on realistic medieval swordplay, technique, strength, and endurance. The project strengthened my understanding of VR development, including motion tracking, interaction design, and the challenges of recreating real-world physics in a virtual environment. I also designed responsive enemy AI to adapt to varying combat scenarios and player skill levels, and creating training exercises to support player learning and progression.


Made in Unity in C# with Unity XR Rig then moved to Meta SDK Rig
