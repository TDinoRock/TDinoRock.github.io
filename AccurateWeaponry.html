Accurate Weaponry

Accurate Weaponry is a physics-driven, open-world VR combat simulator focused on realistic medieval swordplay, technique, strength, and endurance. The project strengthened my understanding of VR development, including motion tracking, interaction design, and the challenges of recreating real-world physics in a virtual environment. I also designed responsive enemy AI to adapt to varying combat scenarios and player skill levels, and creating training exercises to support player learning and progression.


Made in Unity in C# with Unity XR Rig then moved to Meta SDK Rig
