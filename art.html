My Animations

First Rigging - Tip Toe

This was my first character rigging project while learning Autodesk Maya, and it served as an introduction to the fundamentals of rigging and animation. The exercise helped me develop a foundational understanding of joint placement, motion timing, and how subtle movement brings a character to life. Although it is a short animation rather than a full walk cycle, it provided a strong basis for developing more advanced animation skills and understanding the importance of believable motion in character performance.


Made in Maya

First 22 Years

First 22 Years is a rotoscoped short film that follows the first 22 years of a woman’s life. The project required extensive editing and compositing, and it provided valuable experience in production workflow, shot planning, and post-production techniques. Because the footage was not captured against a green screen, the rotoscoping process was significantly more time-intensive, reinforcing the importance of pre-production planning and efficient shooting methods. This project strengthened my understanding of visual storytelling and the technical considerations required to produce a polished final piece.


Made in Adobe Photoshop, Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Blender

Ammonite

Ammonite is a short animated sequence that follows the life of an ammonite from its creation to its display in a museum. The project strengthened my understanding of layered compositing, non-destructive editing, and visual storytelling through motion. It also reinforced the importance of disciplined workflow and organized asset management in post-production, helping me develop a more efficient and polished approach to creating animated narratives.


Made in Adobe Photoshop and Premiere Pro
