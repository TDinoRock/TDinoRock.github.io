VR Brain Rot

Brain Rot is a short VR experience set in a classroom, where the player must remove distracting “brain rot” media while listening to a lecture and achieve a perfect score on a final quiz. The project sparked my interest in VR development and helped me build a foundation in immersive interaction design, including object pickup and snap-based placement mechanics. It strengthened my understanding of user interaction, environmental design, and the creative possibilities of VR as a gameplay platform.


Made in Unity in C# language and with Meta Quest 2
